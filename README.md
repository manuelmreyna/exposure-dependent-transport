# PhEDEx2: Phase Exposure Dependent Exchange and Transport

Code accompanying the manuscript

> **A synthesis of phase exposure dependent parameters and mobile-immobile exchange to represent non-Fickian transport in rivers**
> Manuel Reyna and Timothy R. Ginn

This repository contains the Python notebooks used to build, validate, numerically solve, and calibrate the **PhEDEx2** model: a 1D river transport model in which both the transport parameters (velocity, dispersion) and the exchange rates depend on the time a solute parcel has spent in the mobile phase (age), combined with mobile-immobile exchange. Because the extra age dimension makes grid-based and Laplace-domain solutions impractical at high Péclet number, the model is solved with a **High-Péclet number Perturbation Green's Function (HP-PGF)** method: an expansion in $1/Pe$ that decouples the problem into a sequence of Green's-function convolutions (one per immobile-to-mobile exchange event), with the immobile-phase convolution evaluated by FFT.

## Notebook guide

### `time_dependent_brownian_bridge.ipynb`

Mechanistic derivation of the age-dependent parameters. A solute particle moves in a channel of width $B$ with

$$dX = v(Y)\,dt + \sqrt{2D_L}\,dW_x, \qquad dY = \sqrt{2D_T}\,dW_y \ (\text{reflected at } y=\pm B/2),$$

and a U-shaped transverse profile $v(y) = U\,\frac{a+1}{a}\left(1-|2y/B|^a\right)$.

- `C_y`, `C_y_int`: transverse concentration of the reflected Brownian motion, using the Fourier series for late times and the method of images for early times (switch at $D_T t/B^2 = 1/\pi^2$).
- `v_y`, `Expected_position`, `Effective_Velocity`, `Effective_Dispersion`, `Effective_Dispersion_uniform`: effective velocity and dispersion from the evolution of the first two spatial moments.
- Forward exchange rate $k_{f,\mathrm{eff}}(t)$ from the concentration at the banks.
- Three injection scenarios: **central**, **boundary** (mimicking immobile-to-mobile re-entry) and **uniform**.
- Asymptotic check: $D_\mathrm{eff}(\infty) \propto U^2B^2 / (D_T\, r_a)$ with $r_a = 6(a+3)(2a+3)$ (Aris-Taylor).
- Produces the effective-parameter figures and saves everything to `effective_params.npz`.

### `PhEDEx2.ipynb`

| Section | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Setup / helpers** | Analytical ADE kernels (constant and exponential-in-time parameters), the interpolated mechanistic kernels `ADE_forced_analytical_derived_params`, NRMSE metric (`RMSD_func`), de Hoog numerical Laplace inversion (`invlap`) and the Laplace-space MFE solution (`ADEMF_1D`), analytical MFE moments (`moms_analyt`), and measured moments (`moms_meas`). |
| **Validation** | `run_TSM` is the HP-PGF solver for constant coefficients (first-order or gamma-distributed exchange). Evaluated over all parameter sets estimated for TIERRAS, with $N = 5, 10, 25$ expansion terms. Reports errors in mass conservation, moments 1-3, and NRMSE against Laplace inversion. |
| **Phase exposure dependent** | `run_PhEDEx2` is the full PhEDEx2 solver (age-dependent $v$, $D$, $k_f$). Compares ADE, PhEDEx (MFE), time-dependent ADE and PhEDEx2 in terms of CV, CSK and variance growth with distance for random parameter draws. |
| **Real data** | `load_btcs` and the moment helpers process the TIERRAS BTCs (295 curves). Reproduces the motivation figures: CSK vs CV against ADE / time-dependent ADE predictions, power-law growth of the first four cumulants, discharge changes, and spatial skewness from interpolated inverse CDFs. |
| **Parameter estimation** | Moment-matching of the first four moments with Péclet-number regularization: differential evolution for MFE, Nelder-Mead for PhEDEx2. Applied to the Little Piney Creek and Monocacy River tracer tests, with BTC and cumulant comparison plots. |

### Parameters

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| $Pe = U L / D_\infty$ | Péclet number at sampling distance $L$ |
| $\beta \hat{k}_{f,\infty}$ | Long-time forward exchange rate scaled by the mobile/immobile capacity ratio |
| $\hat{k}_r$ | Dimensionless reverse (immobile to mobile) exchange rate |
| $d = D_L/D_\infty$ | Fraction of longitudinal dispersion not due to transverse shear |
| $\kappa_{bk}$ | Fraction of forward exchange occurring at the banks (the part that is age dependent) |
| $a$ | Exponent of the U-shaped transverse velocity profile |

The MFE reference model has the parameters $[Pe, \beta\hat{k}, \hat{k}]$; PhEDEx2 has $[Pe, \beta\hat{k}_{f,\infty}, \hat{k}_r, d, \kappa_{bk}, a]$.

## Numerical notes

- **Resolution:** the validation runs use $\Delta x/L = \Delta t\,U/L = 1/120$ and integrate to $t = 15\,L/U$.
- **Convergence:** errors in the HP-PGF expansion grow as $\beta\hat{k}$ exceeds ~1 (more terms $N$ are needed; $>25$ for $\beta\hat{k}>10$) and for $Pe \lesssim 50$. At very high $Pe$ ($\gtrsim 10^4$) the error comes from the resolution of the Green's function integration instead.
- **Conservation:** mass conservation (zeroth moment) is checked in all validation cases.
- **Optimizer settings:** PhEDEx2 fits use Nelder-Mead with bounds on all parameters and a log-Péclet regularization toward the hydraulics-based value $Pe_a$; the MFE fits use SciPy's differential evolution with a heavy regularization weight.
